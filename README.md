# Bay City Mirror

## NOT AFFILIATED WITH BIG FINISH GAMES!
"Bay City Mirror" was created in the game ["The Pandora Directive" (1996)](https://bigfinishgames.com/games/the-pandora-directive/). The URL was mentioned in the original game.


## About The Site

When playing ["The Pandora Directive" (1996)](https://bigfinishgames.com/games/the-pandora-directive/), you'll come across a business card of a reporter for the fictional "Bay City Mirror". In addition to working phone and fax numbers, an email address for the character was also on the card. Yet at the time, the URL was not owned by anyone.

Until May 2025 when I bought the domain: [baymirr.com](http://www.baymirr.com)
